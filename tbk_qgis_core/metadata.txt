about=
    A set of processing tools for parametrized creation of stand maps as well as extraction of stand attributes and further processing.
    Input data to delineate forest stand maps are vegetation height models (and forest type rasters).
    Examples for information on the forest (stands) are, amongst others: dominating height/diameter, degree of cover (per layer), conifer proportion, horizontal and vertical structure, local density zones, volume, change of the upper layer (if datasets from at least two dates are available).

    <i>This is the stable core algorithm (replaced since 2026-03 by the modularized design in v0.5.0, allowing more flexible and regionwise processing). Further development on this branch will be ceased in the course of 2026 (consider switching, once the new design is stable). As TBk QGIS core v0.4.2 it can be installed alongside the continuously developed current version.</i>

    The core algorithm and toolkit was developed at the Bern University of Applied Science and is Open Source since 2025. It is designed as an expandable toolkit, contributions are welcome.

    - Hannes Horneber (<a href="mailto:hannes.horneber@bfh.ch">hannes.horneber@bfh.ch</a>) Technical Development

tracker=https://github.com/HAFL-WWI/TBk/issues
